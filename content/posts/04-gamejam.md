This week I participated in a 48h game jam and worked in a team with 4 others. We made
[this Grimm-themed game](https://furiouscivet.itch.io/something-grimm-this-way-comes):

{{< img "images/grimm_gameplay.gif" >}}

My job was to take care of the 3D graphics. I made a painted wood material, some figurines,
the paper material (I didn't draw the map) and took care of the lighting.
I'm very pleased with what we could put together in such a short amount of time.

Actually, I made 5 figurines in total, but due to time constraints, only the player's made it into the game. Here is a mockup of all 5 in the game:

{{< img "images/grimm_all_5.png" >}}

To create the wooden figurines, I first created a procedural painted wood material.
The figurines themselves are just low-poly models with a flat color per face.
